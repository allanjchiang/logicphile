Protein and Nutrient Tracker for Vegetarians and Vegans

App ID: com.logicphile.pohps

Support

POHPS is a free, open-source app designed to help vegetarians and vegans track daily protein, with optional water, iron, calcium and vitamin B12 tracking. There are no ads or paywalls, and every feature is available to everyone. Optional donations are available, but they don't unlock anything.

How to get help

  • Email us at allan@logicphile.com
  • Include your device model, Android/iOS version, and app version (shown in Settings → About POHPS)
  • Describe what you were trying to do and what happened

We aim to respond within a few business days. We cannot provide medical or dietary advice by email—please speak with your doctor or registered dietitian for personal health guidance.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Logicphile Limited (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how the POHPS mobile application handles information.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data on our servers.
  • All app data (daily logs, goals, custom foods, settings) is stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs in the app.
  • There are no accounts, logins, or cloud sync in the current version.

Data stored on your device

May include:

  • Daily food logs, including the time of each entry and, if you turn on the mood tracker, how much you liked each food
  • Daily protein, water, iron and calcium totals calculated from the foods you log
  • Dates you checked off taking vitamin B12, if you turn on the B12 reminder
  • Your daily goals for protein, water, iron and calcium
  • App preferences (language, theme, units, diet type and which trackers are turned on)
  • Custom foods and supplements you create, including their ingredients, and optional protein overrides for certain items
  • Favorite foods and achievements you have unlocked
  • Whether you accepted the in-app disclaimer

This data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data in your device settings. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

Backup and export files

POHPS creates a backup file or an Excel export only when you ask it to. These files are saved or shared to the location you choose, such as your device's storage, a cloud storage service or an email. We never receive a copy. Anything that happens to a file after you save or share it is governed by the service you send it to.

Optional donations

POHPS offers optional in-app donations. Donations do not unlock any features. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment card details or other payment information.

Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or address (unless you voluntarily email support)
  • Location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data
  • Health data transmitted to our servers (we have no servers for app data)

Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive only what you choose to send (e.g. your email address and message content). We use that information only to respond to your request and do not sell it to third parties.

Children

POHPS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
