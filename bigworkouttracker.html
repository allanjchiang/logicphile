A Workout Tracker That Stays Out of Your Way

Big Workout Tracker is designed around a simple, friendly interface, so logging sets, watching your progress, and planning your next session feels effortless every time you open it.

Stay Consistent

A Calendar That Shows Your Consistency

Every workout you log fills in a color on your calendar, so an entire month of training is visible at a glance. Each color represents a workout type you define yourself, making it simple to spot patterns and notice gaps before they turn into missed weeks.

  • ✓ Color-coded days for every workout type you create
  • ✓ Tap any day to see exactly what you trained
  • ✓ Move freely between months to review your history
Track Your Progress

Detailed Statistics For Every Exercise

Your logged sets turn into clear progress charts for weight, reps, and estimated one-rep max. Pick any exercise and any time range, from a single week to your entire training history, to see exactly how your performance has changed.

  • ✓ Interactive charts for weight, reps, and estimated 1RM
  • ✓ Flexible time ranges, from one week to all time
  • ✓ A clear view of trends across every exercise you track
Build Your Routine

Unlimited Workout Templates

Create as many workout templates as your training needs, organized exactly the way you like. Group exercises by muscle, split, or routine, then reorder everything with a simple long-press and drag. When it's time to train, starting a workout takes a single tap.

  • ✓ Create unlimited templates for every routine you follow
  • ✓ Reorder exercises and workouts with long-press and drag
  • ✓ Start any workout instantly from your own list
Train With Context

View Your History Mid-Workout

There's no need to guess what you lifted last time. While you're in the middle of a workout, open the history for any exercise to see your previous sets, reps, and weight right when that information matters most, so you can decide how to progress with confidence.

  • ✓ Pull up past sets and reps without leaving your workout
  • ✓ See your previous best for every exercise at a glance
  • ✓ Make informed decisions about weight and reps in real time
