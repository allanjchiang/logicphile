Big Workout Tracker is a tool to help you record your workouts. You create your own workout templates and exercises, log your sets, reps and weights, and the app keeps a history, a calendar and charts of what you entered. That's it: it records what you tell it and shows it back to you.

Please consult your doctor before starting any exercise program, or before making big changes to how you train. This is especially important if you are pregnant, are recovering from an injury, have a medical condition, or take medication. 3. We don't prescribe exercises

Big Workout Tracker does not recommend, prescribe or plan exercises for you. Every workout, exercise, weight and rep count in the app is something you chose to enter. You are responsible for deciding what is safe for you to do.
