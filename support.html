Privacy Policy

Big Workout Tracker stores your workout and rep entries locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Data remains on your device unless you choose to export it.

If you export data, the export is created by your device and shared only through the method you choose.

On iOS, the app may ask for permission to send notifications, so it can alert you when a rest timer ends while the app is in the background. This is optional and the app works fully without it. These notifications are scheduled on your device and are never sent from a server.

We do not use analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services.
