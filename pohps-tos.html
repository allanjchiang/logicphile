1. What POHPS is

POHPS is a simple tool to help you keep track of the protein you eat each day. You can also choose to track water, iron, calcium and vitamin B12. That's it: POHPS records what you tell it and adds up the numbers for you.

2. Not medical advice

Nothing in POHPS is medical, nutritional or dietary advice. The app doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any health condition, and it doesn't know your personal health needs. Goals and suggestions in the app are general starting points, not recommendations for you specifically.

Please consult your doctor or a registered dietitian before starting any diet, changing how you eat, or taking supplements, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or take medication. Never ignore professional advice or delay getting it because of something you saw in the app.

3. The numbers are estimates

Protein, iron, calcium, vitamin B12 and water values in POHPS are approximate estimates. Real amounts can differ depending on the brand, recipe, how the food is prepared, and how much you actually eat. Please don't rely on POHPS for exact measurements. If precise amounts matter for your health, check food labels and ask a professional.
